1. Your role

As a Reporting Manager you:

  • add, update and remove trainings for your reportees,
  • review their progress on the dashboard,
  • approve completed trainings (or send them back with remarks). A training only counts as complete once you approve it.

Every training must have a training material link before it's assigned. Your reportees see it while they work, and it's required before they can ask for approval.

2. Getting started

  1. HR adds you to Skillgo and you get an email: "You've been invited". Click Accept invitation, click it again on the Skillgo page, then set your password.
  2. From then on, open https://tni-webapp.vercel.app, choose Reporting Manager under Sign in as, and sign in with your email and password.

Forgot your password? Click Forgot your password? on the sign-in screen.

4. Adding your team

Go to Team & Settings.

One reportee

  1. Click Add Reportee, enter their full name and o2h email, pick a colour, and click Add & send invite.
  2. They receive an invite email and set their own password.
If the email can't be sentSkillgo shows "Account ready — but the email couldn't be sent" with a one-time link. Click Copy and send it to the person on Teams or Outlook.

Many reportees at once (Bulk Add)

  1. Click Bulk Add, then Template to download the Excel template (columns: Full Name, Email).
  2. Fill in one person per row, save, and upload the file.
  3. Skillgo checks every row and marks problems (missing name, invalid email, duplicates, people who are already users). Rows with errors are skipped.
  4. Click Add N reportees. Each row then shows "Invite emailed" or "Added — share link". If any emails couldn't be sent, click Copy links to copy all their invite links at once.

Managing reportees

Deactivate / ReactivateBlocks or restores their sign-in. Their trainings and history are kept.

To move someone to another manager or change their role, ask HR. They can do it from the Users page.

5. Training Catalog

The catalog is the shared, company-wide list of trainings. Set a training up once here, then assign it to anyone, singly or in bulk.

Adding a training

  1. Click Add Training.
  2. Fill in Training Name, Training Category (group, then category), Training Mode and Training Priority. For Face to Face trainings, also enter the Trainer. Training Details, extra reference links and Parts / Modules are optional.
  3. The Training Material Link can be left empty in the catalog, but it must be filled in before the training is assigned.
  4. Click Add to catalog.

Bulk import from company sheets

Click Bulk Import and upload one of o2h's training sheets exactly as it is:

  • New Joiner 6 Months Training Plan: topics, details, trainer and method are read, and the category is matched from the section (Human Resource, BA, Quality Assurance, Information Technology).
  • TNI plan: category, method, priority (A+, A, B+, B) and any link in Detail/Comment are read.
  • Any simple sheet with a Training Name column also works.

Every tab in the workbook is read. In the preview, check each training's category (you can change it from the dropdown), untick anything you don't want, then click Import. Trainings already in the catalog are skipped. Personal columns such as Status, Completion Date and signatures are ignored.

Using Google Sheets?Go to File → Download → Microsoft Excel (.xlsx), then upload that file. PDFs can't be read.

Editing and deleting

  • Click the pencil icon to edit a catalog training.
  • Click the bin icon to delete one, or tick several (or Select all) and click Delete N selected.
  • Deleting from the catalog doesn't remove trainings already assigned to people.

6. Assigning trainings

One training to one or more reportees

  1. On the Dashboard click Assign Training, or click Assign next to a training in the catalog.
  2. Under From Training Catalog, pick a training, and its details fill in automatically. Or choose + New training and fill in the fields yourself. Tick "Also save this training to the Training Catalog" to reuse it later.
  3. Set the Expected Training End Date (required) and, optionally, a Due Date. The due date drives overdue flags and reminders.
  4. Tick the reportees (or Select all) and click Assign Training.

Many trainings to many reportees (Bulk Assign)

  1. Click Bulk Assign on the Dashboard, or tick trainings in the catalog and click Assign N selected.
  2. Pick the trainings and the reportees, and set the dates. If a chosen catalog training has no material link, Skillgo asks for one here and saves it to the catalog too.
  3. Click Create N assignments.

After assigning, each reportee gets one email listing their new trainings. A message at the bottom right confirms whether the emails went out. The training is assigned even if an email fails.

Links are checkedMaterial, reference and part links must be real web addresses (for example https://… or docs.google.com/…). Free text in a link box shows "Enter a valid link" and blocks saving.

7. Editing and deleting assigned trainings

  • Edit: open a training (from a reportee's card → View all, or any list) and click Edit to change the name, category, link, mode, trainer, priority or dates. Parts can't be changed once a training is assigned. To change them, delete the training and assign it again.
  • Delete: click Delete in the training details, or the bin icon next to a training in a reportee's list (Dashboard → a reportee's card → View all). This removes it together with any notes and approvals, so use it for trainings added by mistake or for demo purposes.

Only you (their reporting manager) and HR can delete a reportee's training. Reportees can't, not even for trainings they assigned to themselves.

8. Approvals

When a reportee marks a training (or a part) as done, it appears under Approvals with their notes and outcome links.

  • Approve: marks it complete and adds it to the Knowledge Hub. You can add optional remarks, which the reportee sees.
  • Send Back: returns it for changes. Remarks are required so the reportee knows what to fix. They correct it and resubmit.

Multi-part trainings are approved part by part. The training is complete when every part is approved.

9. Dashboard

Top cards

Team Members, Training Units (a multi-part training counts as one unit per part), Completion (percentage of units approved) and Overdue. Click any card to see the list behind it.

Progress trends

Switch between Weekly (last 12 weeks) and Monthly (months of the selected FY):

  • Training activity: units assigned, submitted for approval and completed in each period. Hover over the chart to see the numbers for a date.
  • Active learners: how many of your reportees submitted a training in each period. Use it to see whether people are actually doing their trainings.
  • Where trainings stand: how many trainings are not started, in progress, awaiting approval, sent back, completed or overdue.
  • Completion by category: the share of assigned units completed in each training category.

Every chart has a Table link that shows the same numbers as a table.

People

Each reportee has a card with their completion %, units done, pending and overdue counts, and an achievement badge:

BadgeMeaning

The Year-wise Summary table at the bottom shows each person's completed/total units for every FY, which is useful for appraisals. Click Refresh to load the latest data.

10. Reminders

  1. Set how often to nudge people: every X days while a training is pending, and more often once it's overdue. Click Save Settings.
  2. The reminder(s) due now panel lists who is due a nudge. Click Send & Mark Reminded to open a ready-written email in your mail app, or Copy to paste the text into Teams.

Reminders are not sent automatically. They go out when you click the button.

11. Year-end tools

At the end of the financial year, go to Team & Settings → Year-End Tools. Every unfinished training for the current FY is listed. For each one choose:

  • ↪ Carry to FY [next year]: the training moves to the new year, including its parts.
  • Discard: it's archived and still visible when you look at the old FY.

Then click Finalize FY … → Start FY …. New assignments go into the new year from then on.

12. Management report

Click Export on the Dashboard, tick one or more FYs, and download an Excel file with three sheets: Team Summary (completion % and achievement per person), Training Details (every training and part with status and dates) and Learnings & Notes (approved notes and outcome links).

13. Knowledge Hub

A searchable library of your team's approved trainings, with notes and links. Search by training name or note text, and filter by FY.

14. Your own trainings

If you report to a senior manager, HR sets that up, and My Trainings then appears in your menu. There you work through your own trainings like any reportee: Start, Mark as Done, and Assign to myself to pick catalog trainings. Your senior manager approves them. You can also choose Reportee on the sign-in screen to go straight to My Trainings.

You can't assign, edit, delete or approve your own trainings. Only your senior manager (or HR) can.

15. Frequently asked questions

A reportee didn't get their invite.

Ask them to check Junk / Other. Then click Send setup link. If email still doesn't arrive, copy the link Skillgo shows and send it on Teams.

A reportee assigned a training to themselves. Do I need to do anything?

No. You get an email telling you what they picked. It shows a Self-assigned tag and you approve it as usual. If it isn't relevant, you can delete it.

Can I change a training's parts after assigning it?

No. Delete it and assign it again with the right parts.

Someone is moving to another team.

Ask HR to change their reporting manager on the Users page. Their trainings move with them.

I discarded a training by mistake at year-end.

Discarded trainings are archived, not deleted. Assign the training again from the catalog.
