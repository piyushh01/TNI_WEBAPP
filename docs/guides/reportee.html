1. What is Skillgo?

Skillgo is o2h's tool for tracking your trainings for the financial year (FY). Your reporting manager assigns trainings to you. You can also pick trainings for yourself from the company's Training Catalog. You work through each training, record what you learned, and ask your manager to approve it. A training only counts as complete once your manager approves it. Approved learnings are shared with your team in the Knowledge Hub.

Open Skillgo in any browser at https://tni-webapp.vercel.app. There is nothing to install.

2. First-time sign-in

  1. Your manager or HR adds you to Skillgo, and you receive an email with the subject "You've been invited". If it isn't in your inbox, check the Junk or Other folder.
  2. Click Accept invitation in the email. Skillgo opens with a "Welcome to Skillgo" page. Click Accept invitation again.
  3. Choose your own password (at least 6 characters), type it twice, and click Set password & continue.
  4. You are now signed in and can see My Trainings.
Your password is privateYour manager and HR never see your password. If they send you a new setup link, you choose a new password yourself.
Got a temporary password instead?If your manager or HR sent you your email and a temporary password (for example on Teams), just open Skillgo, pick Reportee, and sign in with it. You'll be asked to choose your own password straight away.
"This link has expired or was already used"Invite links work once and only for a limited time. Ask your manager or HR to click Login access next to your name. You'll get a fresh email, or they can send you the link on Teams.

3. Signing in after that

  1. Open Skillgo. Under Sign in as, choose Reportee.
  2. Enter your o2h email and your password, then click Sign in.

If you pick the wrong role, Skillgo tells you which role your account has. Select the correct one and sign in again.

Forgot your password? Click Forgot your password? on the sign-in screen and enter your email. Open the email you receive, click the link, click Continue, and choose a new password.

4. Your screen

The dark green menu on the left has:

  • My Trainings: your own trainings. The badge shows your progress for the current FY, for example 3/8 means 3 of 8 units are complete.
  • Knowledge Hub: approved learnings from your team.
  • User Guide: opens this guide.
  • Your name, role and Logout at the bottom.

On a phone or small screen, tap the ☰ menu button at the top to open the menu.

5. My Trainings

This is your main screen. The line under the title shows how many units you have completed in the selected FY. A single training is 1 unit. A training with 4 parts is 4 units.

Trainings can arrive in your list without dates, for example when HR or your manager adds a company training plan for you. They show "Not started yet". You choose the dates when you start them (see section 6).

Choosing the financial year

Use the FY dropdown at the top right to see trainings from an earlier year. A training carried over from last year shows a small ↪ FY 2025-26 tag.

6. Starting a training

  1. Find the training in My Trainings and click Start.
  2. Choose the Start date and the End date (when you plan to finish; both are required), then click Start training. The status changes to In Progress and the dates appear on the training. Your manager can also do this for you: it's called Initiate on their side.
  3. Open the study material from Details (the "Training Material" link), or from the link on the training.
  4. For a single training, pick how far along you are from the % done dropdown on the training (0%–100%) as you go. Your manager sees this progress. It is only a guide and doesn't complete the training. Multi-part trainings show progress by the parts you complete (see section 8).

7. Completing a training

  1. When you've finished, click Mark as Done.
  2. Fill in Key Learnings / Outcome Notes: what you learned and how you'll use it. Be specific, because your teammates will read this in the Knowledge Hub.
  3. Under Outcome Reference Material, add at least one link to what you produced: your notes document, a certificate, a recording and so on. Give each link a short title. Click Add another reference to add more.
  4. Click Submit for Approval. The status changes to Awaiting Approval.
Links must be real web linksThe link field only accepts a web address such as https://docs.google.com/… or sharepoint.com/…. Plain text like "saved in my drive" is rejected with the message "Enter a valid link", and the submit button stays disabled until it's fixed.

What happens next

  • Approved: the training shows Completed, counts in your progress and appears in the Knowledge Hub. Any remarks from your manager appear in Details.
  • Sent back: the status shows Sent Back with your manager's remarks. Make the changes and click Resubmit.

8. Multi-part trainings

Some trainings are split into parts or modules. They show a badge like 0/3 Parts and a progress bar.

  1. Click Start on the training first. You can't mark parts as done before starting it.
  2. Click the training row (or the ▼ arrow) to expand the list of parts.
  3. When you finish a part, click Mark as Done next to that part and submit notes and at least one link, just like a single training.
  4. Your manager approves each part separately. The training becomes Completed when every part is approved.

You can complete parts in any order, but they usually build on each other, so it's best to go in sequence. For an approved part, click View Notes to see what you submitted.

9. Assigning trainings to yourself

You can pick trainings from the company Training Catalog and add them to your own list. You can't create new trainings, only choose ones already in the catalog.

  1. In My Trainings, click Assign to myself.
  2. Search the catalog and tick one or more trainings.
  3. Optionally choose an Expected End Date and a Due Date. You can leave them empty and set the dates when you start the training.
  4. Click Assign to me. The trainings appear in your list with a Self-assigned tag, and your manager gets an email telling them what you picked.

A catalog training you're already working on can't be picked again. It shows "Already in your list".

Approval works the same waySelf-assigned trainings are completed exactly like assigned ones. Start the training, mark it as done, and your manager approves it. Only your manager or HR can delete a training from your list.

10. Training details

Click Details on any training to see everything about it: category, mode (online, face to face, self-paced or blended), trainer, priority, expected end and due dates, the study material and any extra reference links. Once a training is completed, the details also show your notes, your outcome links and your manager's remarks.

11. Knowledge Hub

The Knowledge Hub is a searchable library of approved trainings from you and your teammates (people with the same reporting manager). Use it to:

  • see what a training covers before you start it,
  • read a teammate's notes and the links they found useful.

Search by training name or by words in anyone's notes, and use the FY dropdown to filter by year. Click a card to see everyone who completed that training, with their notes and links.

13. Frequently asked questions

I didn't get the invite email.

Check the Junk / Other folder first. If it isn't there, ask your manager or HR to click Login access next to your name. If email still doesn't arrive, they can copy the link and send it to you on Teams.

It says "This account is registered as … not Reportee".

You selected the wrong role on the sign-in screen. Choose the role shown in the message and sign in again.

It says my account has been deactivated.

Your manager or HR has paused your access. Contact them.

A training in my list has no dates.

That's normal for trainings added from a plan. Click Start when you begin it and choose the start and end dates.

Why can't I click Mark as Done?

Start the training first. On a multi-part training, start the training before marking parts as done.

Why is Submit for Approval greyed out?

Both fields are required: notes, and at least one valid link. Look for a red "Enter a valid link" message under a link box.

I made a mistake in my notes.

While a training is waiting for approval, ask your manager to send it back. You can then correct it and resubmit. After approval, notes can't be edited.

Can I delete a training from my list?

No. Only your reporting manager or HR can remove a training, including ones you assigned to yourself. Ask them if something was added by mistake.

My progress on the dashboard looks different from what I expected.

Progress is counted in units. Each part of a multi-part training is one unit, and only approved units count as done.
