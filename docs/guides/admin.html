1. Your role

As Admin / HR you look after Skillgo for the whole organisation:

  • People: create managers and reportees, set who reports to whom, and control access.
  • Visibility: see everyone's training progress, how many trainings each manager has approved, and what is overdue. This is view only.
  • Catalog & plans: maintain the company-wide Training Catalog and assign trainings to people, for example a new joiner's plan straight from the sheet.
  • Clean-up: delete trainings that were assigned by mistake.

Approving completions stays with each person's Reporting Manager.

2. Signing in

  1. Open https://tni-webapp.vercel.app.
  2. Under Sign in as, choose Admin / HR, enter your email and password, and click Sign in.
Change the starting passwordIf you were given a shared or default password, change it straight away: sign out, click Forgot your password?, and follow the email link.

4. Users: adding people

  1. Go to Users and click Add User.
  2. Enter the Full name and Email, and choose a Role:
    • Reportee: must have a Reporting Manager.
    • Reporting Manager: a Reporting Manager is optional. Set one if this manager also reports to someone (see section 6).
    • Admin / HR: no reporting manager.
  3. Choose how they get their login: Send invite email (they set their own password from the email), or Temporary password (no email; you share their email and password yourself, and they must change it after signing in).
  4. Click Add & send invite or Add & create password. Copy message to send copies a ready-written message with the login details.

Add Reporting Managers before their reportees, so the manager can be picked from the list.

If the email can't be sentSkillgo shows "Account ready — but the email couldn't be sent" with a one-time link. Click Copy and share it on Teams or Outlook.

5. Users: bulk add

  1. On Users, click Bulk Add, then Template.
  2. Fill in the Users sheet, one person per row:
    ColumnWhat to enter
    Full Namee.g. Priya Shah
    Emailtheir o2h email
    RoleReportee, Reporting Manager or Admin / HR (blank = Reportee)
    Reporting Manager EmailRequired for reportees: the email of an existing manager, or of a manager added in the same sheet
  3. Upload the file. Each row is checked for a missing name, invalid email, duplicates in the sheet, people who are already users, unknown roles, and a missing or unknown reporting manager. Rows with problems are listed with the reason and skipped.
  4. Choose Send invite email or Temporary password, then click Add N users. Managers in the sheet are created first, so their reportees can be linked to them straight away. Each row shows "Invite emailed" or "Added — share link".
  5. If any invites couldn't be emailed, or you chose temporary passwords, click Copy links / Copy login details to copy everyone's details, with names, in one go.

Reporting managers can also bulk-add their own reportees from Team & Settings.

6. Roles & reporting lines

Click the pencil icon next to a user to change their name, role or reporting manager.

A manager who also reports to someone

Keep their role as Reporting Manager and set their Reporting Manager to their senior. They then:

  • keep their own team, dashboard and approvals,
  • get My Trainings for their own trainings, which their senior assigns and approves,
  • can choose Reportee on the sign-in screen to go straight to their own trainings.

Admin / HR as a reporting manager

Admin / HR users also appear in the Reporting Manager list, marked (Admin / HR). For example, you can set an HR user as a senior manager's reporting manager. That HR user then gets an Approvals tab to approve their direct reportees' completions. As HR, they can already assign trainings to them.

Moving someone to another manager

Edit the person and pick the new Reporting Manager. Their existing trainings move with them, and the new manager can approve them.

Rules Skillgo enforces

  • A reportee must always have a reporting manager.
  • Reporting lines can't loop (A reports to B while B reports to A).
  • A manager who still has reportees can't be changed to another role or deleted. Move their reportees first.
  • You can't remove your own Admin role or delete yourself.

7. Access: setup links, deactivate, delete

Status / actionWhat it means
ActiveHas set a password and can sign in
Invite pendingHasn't set their password yet
InactiveSign-in blocked (deactivated)
Login accessThree options: Email a setup link, Copy a setup link (no email, share it on Teams) or Set a temporary password (no email, share their email and password). Use it for expired or missing invites and forgotten passwords. A new link or password replaces the previous one.
Deactivate / ReactivateBlocks or restores sign-in (with a confirmation step). All their data is kept. Use this when someone leaves or goes on long leave.
Delete (bin icon)Permanently removes the person with all their trainings, notes and approvals. It can't be undone. Trainings they had assigned to others are kept and re-owned by you.

8. Overview

The Overview is your organisation-wide picture. Use the filters at the top to narrow it to one manager's team and a financial year. Everything on the page follows those filters.

Cards

Employees, Trainings assigned, Completed, In progress, Awaiting approval (waiting on managers) and Overdue.

Progress trends

Switch between Weekly and Monthly:

  • Training activity: units assigned, submitted and completed per period. Hover to see exact numbers.
  • Active learners: how many people submitted a training in each period, which tells you whether people are actually doing their trainings.
  • Where trainings stand: counts by status.
  • Completion by category: the share of assigned units completed per training category.

Click Table on any chart to see the numbers as a table.

Managers table

For each manager: team size, trainings assigned and completed, approvals given, trainings sent back, trainings awaiting approval (their backlog), overdue, and completion %.

Employees table

For each person: their manager, trainings assigned, not started, in progress, awaiting approval, completed, overdue and a progress bar. Click View to see their trainings. Click any training for full details, or click the bin icon (or Delete in the details) to remove a training that was added by mistake.

Trainings — who is doing what

At the bottom of the Overview, two views follow the same filters:

  • By training: each training with how many people have it assigned, not started, in progress, awaiting approval, completed and overdue. Click a training to see every person on it, with their dates, progress and status.
  • All assignments: one row per person per training, showing manager, category, status, start → end dates, due date, progress and completion date.

Search by training or person name, and filter by status or category. Click any row to open the full training details.

Export

Click Export to download an Excel report for the people currently shown: Team Summary, Training Details, and Learnings & Notes.

9. Training Catalog

The catalog is shared by all managers. Reportees can also pick from it to assign trainings to themselves.

  • Add Training: name, category, mode (with trainer for Face to Face), priority, optional details, reference links and parts. The material link is optional. Tick people under Assign to to give it to them straight away, as "Not started".
  • Assign / Assign N selected: assign catalog trainings to people at any time. Dates are optional. The person, or their manager, sets the start and end dates when starting the training. Each person is emailed about their new trainings.
  • Bulk Import: upload a New Joiner 6 Months Training Plan or TNI plan sheet as it is (.xlsx or .csv; from Google Sheets use File → Download → Microsoft Excel). Check the categories in the preview and untick what you don't need. If the sheet names the person it's for, Skillgo pre-selects them under Assign these trainings to. Click Import & assign to add the trainings to the catalog and assign them in one go. Trainings already in the catalog are just assigned.
  • Edit with the pencil icon. Delete with the bin icon, or tick several and click Delete N selected. Deleting from the catalog doesn't affect trainings already assigned.
Links are checked everywhereLink fields accept only real web addresses (for example https://… or docs.google.com/…). Plain text shows "Enter a valid link".

10. Invite emails & links

  • Invite and password links work once and for a limited time. If someone sees "This link has expired or was already used", click Login access next to their name.
  • Links first open a Skillgo page with an Accept invitation / Continue button. This stops Outlook's link scanner from using up the link before the person clicks it.
  • Emails can land in Junk / Other. Ask people to check there.
  • Reportees get an email when trainings are assigned to them, and managers get one when a reportee assigns themselves a training.

11. Frequently asked questions

How do I set up a new team from scratch?

Add the Reporting Manager first, then their reportees (or put both in one Bulk Add sheet). The manager then assigns trainings from the catalog.

Can HR assign trainings or approve them?

HR can assign trainings, from the catalog or while importing a plan. Approving completions stays with the person's reporting manager. HR can also delete trainings added by mistake.

An invite email never arrived (or went to quarantine).

Open Login access for the person and choose Copy a setup link or Set a temporary password, then send the copied message on Teams. Ask IT to allow the Skillgo sender if emails keep landing in quarantine.

Someone left the company.

Deactivate them to keep their training history for records. Only Delete if the account was created by mistake.

A manager is leaving but has a team.

Edit each reportee and choose their new reporting manager, then deactivate the manager.

Why can't I delete a manager?

They still have reportees. Move the reportees to another manager first.
